Events and Activities







Community-based education, cultural activities, leadership, and spaces for collective learning and participation.

Community Activities



Weekly

Escuela Yemayá Community Encounters

Weekly encounters that bring together children, adolescents, young people, families, community educators, and tradition bearers through community-based and intergenerational education.

Cultural Education

Art, Music, Dance and Oral Tradition

Artistic and cultural workshops that promote Afro-Colombian identity through traditional Pacific dance, Afro-rooted urban dances, African music, literature, collective creation, oral tradition, and traditional instruments.

Community Leadership

Leadership and Community Formation

Spaces for dialogue and learning focused on human rights, anti-racism, youth leadership, community participation, and territorial peacebuilding.

Previous Events and Activities



FUCISPAC and Escuela Yemayá have participated in community, cultural, academic, and educational spaces where Afro-Colombian identity, ancestral knowledge, education, leadership, and community transformation are shared and strengthened.



Community

Community and Intergenerational Gatherings

Spaces that bring together children, young people, families, older adults, community educators, and tradition bearers to share experiences, knowledge, stories, and cultural practices.

Academic Spaces

Forums, Panels and Seminars

Escuela Yemayá has been presented in local, district, national, and international forums, panels, seminars, and academic gatherings focused on identity, racism, cultural resistance, education, and community knowledge.

Knowledge Sharing

Community Knowledge and Research

Universities, research groups, and centers of thought have visited and documented the experience, creating dialogue between academic knowledge and community knowledge in areas including popular education, cultural studies, gender, sociology, anthropology, and human rights.

Cultural Memory

Cultural and Ancestral Knowledge Activities

Activities include circles of dialogue, traditional knowledge sharing, music, dance, oral history, ancestral medicine, and other practices that contribute to the preservation of Afro-Colombian cultural memory.
