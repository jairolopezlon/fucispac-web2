About FUCISPAC

Building identity, preserving ancestral knowledge, and empowering Afro-Colombian communities through education, culture, and community leadership.

Foundation History

2009

Foundation Established

FUCISPAC was founded in August 2009 as a community-based organization committed to preserving Afro-Colombian culture, strengthening ethnic identity, and promoting educational opportunities for children, youth, women, and older adults in Bogotá.

2009

Community Education Begins

The foundation launched educational reinforcement programs, Afro-Colombian Studies initiatives, leadership workshops, women's empowerment activities, ancestral medicine practices, and educational support for adults completing primary and secondary education.

2014

Creation of Escuela Yemayá

FUCISPAC created Escuela Yemayá, a community-based educational initiative in the locality of Usme in Bogotá. This is a program that promotes Afro-Colombian identity through dance, music, oral tradition, leadership, and popular education.

2020

Community Support During the Pandemic

During the COVID-19 pandemic, FUCISPAC organized community support initiatives, women's healing circles, solidarity campaigns, and partnerships with universities, organizations, and volunteers to assist families in need.

Today

Growing Community Impact

Today, FUCISPAC continues expanding its educational, cultural, and community-based programs while working toward the creation of a permanent Afro-Colombian Community Center.

Our Mission

To transform the lives of Afro-Colombian children, youth, and women through education, cultural preservation, and community empowerment, promoting social justice and equity. Also, to promote the recognition of Afro-Colombian identity by valuing the contributions of Afro-Colombian culture to society while strengthening community participation, supporting early childhood development, providing educational reinforcement, and fostering ethical, environmental, and cultural values. Through self-recognition and knowledge of Afro-descendant history, we contribute to the holistic development of Afro-Colombian communities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a leading foundation in building a society where Afro-Colombian communities enjoy full rights, opportunities, and recognition of their cultural identity. We also seek to establish an Afro-Colombian Community Center in Bogotá that offers academic, cultural, artistic, social, environmental, and community programs for children, youth, women, and families. We aspire to create a safe space where Afro-Colombian heritage is celebrated, leadership is nurtured, and future generations grow with pride in their identity while contributing positively to their communities. We are committed to preparing young people to become highly qualified professionals and the next generation of social, political, and cultural leaders. Our goal is to empower them to create meaningful change and represent Afro-Colombian communities at both the national and global levels.

Institutional Values

Cultural Identity

Cultural Identity

We celebrate, preserve, and strengthen Afro-Colombian heritage, traditions, and collective memory.

Value

Social Justice

We promote equity, inclusion, human dignity, and equal opportunities for Afro-descendant communities.

Value

Community Leadership

We empower children, youth, women, and families to become active leaders in their communities, fostering solidarity, mutual support, and shared responsibility to strengthen communities and promote peaceful coexistence.

Value

Ancestral Knowledge

We recognize traditional knowledge, oral history, and ancestral practices as essential sources of learning and community well-being.

Our Approach

We work from an ethnic-racial approach that recognizes and values the identity of Afro-Colombian communities. Our gender perspective ensures that women's rights and leadership are central to all our programs. We are guided by human rights principles, and our methodology is based on community-based work, where beneficiaries are active agents of their own development.
