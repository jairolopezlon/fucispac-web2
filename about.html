About FUCISPAC

Cultural Foundation for Social Integration and Community Participation.

Building identity, preserving ancestral knowledge, and empowering Afro-Colombian communities through education, culture, and community leadership.

Historia de la Fundación

2009

Foundation Established

FUCISPAC was founded in August 2009 as a community-based organization committed to preserving Afro-Colombian culture, strengthening ethnic identity, and promoting educational opportunities for children, youth, women, and older adults in Bogotá.

2009

Community Education Begins

The foundation launched educational reinforcement programs, Afro-Colombian Studies initiatives, leadership workshops, women's empowerment activities, ancestral medicine practices, and educational support for adults completing primary and secondary education.

2014

Creation of Escuela Yemayá

FUCISPAC created Escuela Yemayá, a community-based educational initiative in the locality of Usme in Bogotá. This is a program that promotes Afro-Colombian identity through dance, music, oral tradition, leadership, and popular education.

2020

Community Support During the Pandemic

During the COVID-19 pandemic, FUCISPAC organized community support initiatives, women's healing circles, solidarity campaigns, and partnerships with universities, organizations, and volunteers to assist families in need.

Today

Growing Community Impact

Today, FUCISPAC continues expanding its educational, cultural, and community-based programs while working toward the creation of a permanent Afro-Colombian Community Center.

Our Mission

Our mission is to transform the lives of Afro-Colombian children, youth, women, and families through education, cultural preservation, and community empowerment, promoting social justice and equity. We also promote the recognition of Afro-Colombian identity by valuing the contributions of Afro-Colombian culture to society while strengthening community participation, supporting early childhood development, providing educational reinforcement, and fostering ethical, environmental, and cultural values. Through self-recognition and knowledge of Afro-descendant history, we contribute to the holistic development of Afro-Colombian communities while creating opportunities for sustainable community development.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a society where Afro-Colombian communities enjoy full rights, opportunities, and recognition of their cultural identity. We envision an Afro-Colombian Community Center in Bogotá that provides academic, cultural, artistic, social, environmental, and community programs for children, youth, women, and families. We aspire to create a safe space where Afro-Colombian heritage is celebrated, leadership is nurtured, and young people are prepared to become professionals and the next generation of social, political, and cultural leaders, creating meaningful change and representing Afro-Colombian communities nationally and globally.

Valores Institucionales

Cultural Identity

Cultural Identity

We celebrate, preserve, and strengthen Afro-Colombian heritage, traditions, and collective memory. We promote pride in cultural identity and create spaces where Afro-Colombian history, cultural expressions, and contributions are recognized, valued, and shared with future generations.

Value

Social Justice

We promote equity, inclusion, human dignity, and equal opportunities for Afro-descendant communities. We challenge discrimination and structural inequalities while advancing rights, participation, and well-being, creating pathways toward a more just, inclusive, and equitable society.

Value

Community Impact

We empower children, youth, women, and families to become active leaders in their communities. We foster solidarity, mutual support, and participation, developing skills and opportunities that strengthen communities and promote positive social, cultural, and political transformation.

Value

Ancestral Knowledge

We recognize traditional knowledge, oral history, and ancestral practices as essential sources of learning and community well-being. We honor the wisdom of elders and communities while promoting intergenerational knowledge sharing, resilience, and collective identity.

Nuestro Enfoque

Trabajamos desde un enfoque étnico-racial que reconoce y valora la identidad de las comunidades afrocolombianas. Nuestra perspectiva de género garantiza que los derechos y el liderazgo de las mujeres sean centrales en todos nuestros programas. Nos guían los principios de derechos humanos, y nuestra metodología se basa en el trabajo comunitario, donde los beneficiarios son agentes activos de su propio desarrollo.
