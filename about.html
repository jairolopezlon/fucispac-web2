About FUCISPAC

Our history, identity, and commitment to Afro-Colombian communities.

Foundation History

2010

Foundation Creation

FUCISPAC was established by community leaders committed to Afro-Colombian rights and development.

2013

First Educational Programs

Launch of after-school tutoring and cultural identity workshops for children and youth in Cali.

2016

Women's Empowerment Initiative

Creation of leadership and economic autonomy programs for Afro-Colombian women.

2020

Community Expansion

Extended programs to rural communities, reaching over 500 families across Valle del Cauca.

2024

Ongoing Impact

Continued growth with new partnerships and projects benefiting children, youth, and women.

Our Mission

To transform the lives of Afro-Colombian children, youth, and women through education, cultural preservation, and community empowerment, promoting social justice and equity.

rocket_launch

Our Vision

To be a leading foundation in building a society where Afro-Colombian communities enjoy full rights, opportunities, and recognition of their cultural identity.

Institutional Values

Value

Value

Active involvement of community members in decision-making and program development.

Our Approach

We work from an ethnic-racial approach that recognizes and values the identity of Afro-Colombian communities. Our gender perspective ensures that women's rights and leadership are central to all our programs. We are guided by human rights principles, and our methodology is based on community-based work, where beneficiaries are active agents of their own development.
