Frequently Asked Questions







Learn more about FUCISPAC, our community programs, and how you can participate and support our work.

What does FUCISPAC do? add

FUCISPAC works to strengthen Afro-Colombian communities through education, cultural preservation, community participation, leadership, and the recognition of Afro-Colombian identity and ancestral knowledge.


Its work includes educational, cultural, artistic, community, and women's empowerment processes that involve children, adolescents, young people, women, families, and community members.

Who does FUCISPAC work with? add

FUCISPAC primarily works with Afro-Colombian communities, including children, adolescents, young people, women, families, caregivers, community leaders, educators, and knowledge holders.


The organization promotes intergenerational learning and community participation, recognizing that different generations contribute valuable knowledge and experiences to collective development.

What is Escuela Yemayá? add

Escuela Yemayá is a community-based educational process created in 2014 in Usme, Bogotá. It is a space for learning, memory, cultural identity, and community transformation.

The school uses dance, music, oral tradition, literature, traditional games, art, and ancestral knowledge to strengthen Afro-Colombian identity and create safe spaces for children, adolescents, young people, and their families.

What activities are part of Escuela Yemayá? add

Escuela Yemayá develops activities related to community education, dance, music, oral tradition, traditional games, literature, artistic expression, leadership, and the preservation of Afro-Colombian cultural knowledge.


Its weekly encounters combine artistic workshops, dialogue circles, leadership activities, and community presentations.

What is the Women's Project? add

FUCISPAC's Women's Project creates spaces for young women, caregivers, and Afro-Colombian women leaders to strengthen their self-esteem, recognize their rights, share their experiences, and build networks of support and collective care.


The process includes activities related to healing, Black feminism, empowerment, and the creation of Afro dolls, while promoting a decolonial, anti-racist, and intersectional approach.

How can I participate in FUCISPAC projects? add

You can participate by becoming a volunteer, supporting community activities, contributing your knowledge and skills, participating in educational or cultural processes, or contacting FUCISPAC to explore opportunities for collaboration.

Can I volunteer with FUCISPAC? add

Yes. Volunteers can contribute to educational, cultural, artistic, community, and leadership activities. FUCISPAC values people who are willing to share their knowledge, skills, time, and experience with the community.


Volunteers may support activities related to education, Afro-Colombian culture, community leadership, ancestral knowledge, arts, creative activities, and collective care.

Does FUCISPAC offer internships? add

Yes. FUCISPAC offers opportunities for internships and community-based learning. University students can participate in the organization's processes and may receive university certification. The minimum internship period is six months.

Where does FUCISPAC work? add

One of FUCISPAC's principal community processes is Escuela Yemayá, which was created in the locality of Usme in Bogotá.


FUCISPAC develops community-based processes focused on Afro-Colombian identity, education, culture, women, families, leadership, and ancestral knowledge.

When does Escuela Yemayá meet? add

Escuela Yemayá holds weekly community encounters. These meetings combine artistic workshops, dialogue circles, leadership activities, and community presentations.

Specific schedules and locations can be confirmed directly with FUCISPAC.

What are FUCISPAC's main projects? add

FUCISPAC's projects include Escuela Yemayá and its community-based educational processes, as well as projects focused on women.


Escuela Yemayá includes processes such as Ye-Mawimbi, oral tradition, and traditional games. The Women's Project includes Afro dolls, healing, and women's empowerment.

How can I support FUCISPAC? add

You can support FUCISPAC by volunteering, sharing your knowledge and skills, participating in community activities, supporting its projects, or making a donation.


Donations help strengthen the community processes and educational, cultural, and social activities developed by the foundation.
