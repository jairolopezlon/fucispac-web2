Frequently Asked Questions

What does FUCISPAC do? add

FUCISPAC is a foundation dedicated to transforming the lives of Afro-Colombian children, youth, and women through education, cultural preservation, and community empowerment programs.

How can I support the organization? add

You can support us by making a donation, volunteering your time and skills, or partnering with us as a corporate sponsor. Visit our Donations page for more information.

How can I participate in the projects? add

You can participate by joining as a volunteer, attending our community events, or contacting us directly to explore collaboration opportunities in your area of interest.

Where does FUCISPAC work? add

FUCISPAC primarily works in Cali and the Valle del Cauca region of Colombia, focusing on Afro-Colombian communities.

Is my donation tax-deductible? add

FUCISPAC is a registered nonprofit organization. Donations may be tax-deductible in Colombia. Please contact us for specific information regarding tax receipts.
