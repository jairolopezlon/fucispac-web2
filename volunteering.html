Why Volunteer with FUCISPAC?



FUCISPAC's work is sustained through community participation, volunteer educators, cultural leaders, and people committed to strengthening Afro-Colombian communities in Bogotá.

Through volunteering, you can contribute to educational, cultural, artistic, and community processes that support children, young people, women, families, and older adults.

Our community work recognizes the importance of Afro-Colombian identity, ancestral knowledge, intergenerational learning, leadership, and collective care.

Ways to Contribute



Education

Educational Support

Support educational reinforcement for children and young people, creating learning opportunities that contribute to educational continuity, personal development, confidence, and future opportunities.

Culture

Afro-Colombian Culture

Contribute to activities that strengthen Afro-Colombian identity through dance, music, literature, oral tradition, and other cultural expressions that celebrate heritage and promote cultural pride.

Leadership

Community Leadership

Support leadership development and community participation, encouraging children and young people to build skills, take active roles, and become agents of positive change in their communities.

Ancestral Knowledge

Traditional Knowledge

Support the preservation and intergenerational transmission of ancestral knowledge through oral tradition, traditional practices, medicinal plants, and community learning that strengthen cultural identity and continuity.

Community

Community Activities

Participate in community gatherings, dialogue circles, intergenerational activities, and spaces that promote collective care, active listening, solidarity, participation, and stronger connections among community members.

Arts

Arts and Creative Activities

Contribute to creative activities such as drawing, literature, theater, music, and dance, creating meaningful opportunities for children, young people and women to express themselves and develop their creativity.

Volunteer with Escuela Yemayá



Escuela Yemayá is a community-based educational process created in 2014 in Usme, Bogotá. It uses popular education, art, ancestral knowledge, and Afro-Colombian cultural practices to strengthen identity, leadership, and community participation.

The school works with children, adolescents, young people, and their families through weekly encounters that combine artistic workshops, circles of dialogue, leadership training, and community presentations.

Volunteers are an important part of this process. Many of the school's educators and facilitators are former participants who continue to share the knowledge and legacy of Escuela Yemayá.

Volunteering with Yemayá means contributing to a space where learning takes place through dialogue between generations and where culture, creativity, identity, and community care are central to the educational process.
