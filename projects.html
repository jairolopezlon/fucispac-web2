Our Projects






Community-based projects that strengthen Afro-Colombian identity, cultural memory, education, ancestral knowledge, women's leadership, and collective transformation.

Escuela Yemayá



Escuela Yemayá is a community-based educational process created in 2014 in Usme, Bogotá. Through art, oral tradition, music, dance, ancestral knowledge, and popular education, it strengthens Afro-Colombian identity, community participation, and intergenerational learning.




Education

Escuela Yemayá

Escuela Yemayá is a space for learning, memory, and transformation that strengthens Afro-Colombian identity through dance, music, oral tradition, art, and community education. It provides a safe space where children, adolescents, young people, and their families can recognize their roots, strengthen their sense of belonging, and build community.

YE-MAWIMBI

YE-MAWIMBI is the dance school connected to the Escuela Yemayá process. It contributes to the preservation and strengthening of Afro-Colombian cultural identity through dance, artistic expression, and collective learning.

Ancestral Knowledge

Oral Tradition

The oral tradition project contributes to the recovery, preservation, and transmission of Afro-Colombian memories, stories, knowledge, and cultural practices. It recognizes oral knowledge as an important source of learning and intergenerational connection.

Traditional Games

Women's Community Project







FUCISPAC's women's community project creates spaces where Afro-Colombian women, young women, caregivers, and community leaders can strengthen their self-esteem, recognize their rights, share experiences, heal, and build networks of support. The process incorporates decolonial, anti-racist, and intersectional perspectives and promotes women's leadership and collective care.



Cultural Identity

Afro Dolls

The Afro Dolls project uses creative work as a way to recognize and celebrate Afro-Colombian identity. Through the creation of Afro dolls, participants strengthen cultural representation, self-recognition, creativity, and pride in Afro-descendant identity.

Healing

Healing

The healing process provides Afro-Colombian women with spaces to share experiences, strengthen their well-being, recognize their own knowledge, and build networks of mutual support. It contributes to collective care and to processes of healing through community, dialogue, and ancestral knowledge.

Learn more
Women's Leadership

Women's Empowerment

This process strengthens the leadership of Afro-Colombian women by promoting recognition of their rights, knowledge, experiences, and contributions. Through a decolonial, anti-racist, and intersectional approach, participants develop tools to identify and confront gender- and race-based violence and become agents of change in their families and communities.
