Our Work

FUCISPAC develops community-based educational, cultural, and leadership processes with children, young people, women, families, and older adults, including the work of Escuela Yemayá in Usme, Bogotá.

Let's Build Community Together

FUCISPAC believes in the power of community participation, cultural identity, ancestral knowledge, education, and collective action to transform territories and strengthen Afro-Colombian communities.
