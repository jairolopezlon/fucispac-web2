Empowering Afro-Colombian Communities through Education, Culture, and Leadership










We are a foundation committed to the integral development of Afro-descendant children, youth, women, and families through education, culture, and community empowerment, strengthening identity, leadership, and opportunities for community transformation.

Learn about our work Support our mission



Who We Are

FUCISPAC was founded with the mission of transforming the lives of Afro-Colombian communities. We work hand in hand with children, youth, and women, promoting education, protecting rights, and strengthening cultural identity. Our principles are rooted in equity, social justice, and community participation.
